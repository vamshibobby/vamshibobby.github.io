  • System-1 Orchestration for Enterprise Conversational Agents: Typed Decisions Beside the LLM on τ²-Bench Airline

    Preprint, November 2026 · Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24501211

    A typed decision model (Jev) running beside the agent's LLM on τ²-Bench Airline let code issue 77–84% of tool calls, cut model calls per turn by 24–37%, raised pass4 on all three agent models (+10.7 points on average; 0.220 → 0.420 on Gemma 4 26B A4B) and cut conversations with a policy-forbidden write from 43 to 10.

  • Choosing Affordable Language Models for Serial Fiction: Instruction Following, Creative Writing, and Story Memory

    Preprint, September 2026 · Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22977925

    Across 687 chapter-generation attempts with six models priced below $1 per million tokens, detailed causal briefs raised reference-event coverage from 4.7% (vague) to 90.7%, numbering the same instructions gave 88.9% with no consistent gain, and the best model depended on the role: GLM 5.3 Flash for balanced creative scenes, DeepSeek V3.2 for detailed plans, Tencent Hy3 (reasoning off) for dependable low-cost drafts.

  • Decisions Without Generation: What Universal Decision Models Changed About Classification, and Where a Fine-Tuned 4B Model Still Wins

    Preprint, November 2026 · Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24601222

    On sixteen datasets, a Qwen3.5-4B fine-tuned with a few thousand labels in ten to eighty GPU-minutes matched or beat the hosted universal decision model Jev on every agentic decision measured, at 55–85 ms per decision against roughly 250 ms and $1–9 against $10–144 per million decisions, while Jev remained the strongest zero-label decision model.

  • BANKING77 Is Broken: Rethinking a Cornerstone Intent-Detection Benchmark for Enterprise Conversational Agents

    Preprint, November 2026 · Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24601220

    An audit of all 13,083 BANKING77 queries finds about 1.1% clearly wrong labels and a further ~6% genuinely ambiguous ones (a realistic ceiling of 96–97%); on the new 20,381-record BANKING77-X, a classifier that scores 0.91 on clean BANKING77 queries drops to 0.48, while the best decision models reach 0.88 and the best open model 0.77.
